pinheat

Hover over the map.
View
Wheel or double-click: zoom · drag: pan · click: select a PIN · Shift+drag: select a rectangle.
Projection
Which digit pairs form the axes (y × x). In AB × CD the rows are the first two digits and the columns the last two. Same PINs, different arrangement; the axis labels show the pattern (47nn = first digits 4 and 7).
Colour
Palette of the colour scale shown under the map. Viridis and cividis stay readable for colour-blind people.
Scale
How the number of occurrences of a PIN becomes a colour.
Exponent of the gamma scale. Below 1 brightens rare PINs, above 1 darkens them.
Linear and gamma only. PINs above this percentile of the count distribution all get the brightest colour, so a few giants such as 1234 do not wash out the rest. 99% = the top 1% of PINs saturate; 100% = no clipping.
Tags (the rest is dimmed)
Show only PINs that match any ticked tag. The percentages are shares of all occurrences; tags can overlap.
By popularity
Highlights the N most (or least) common PINs; the rest is dimmed. The line below says how much of all occurrences they cover.
Selection
Click a PIN to add or remove it; Shift+drag selects a rectangle (replacing the selection). Everything outside the selection is dimmed, and the selection survives a change of projection.
These are passwords from data breaches (HIBP), not a representative sample of PINs in use. Counts are occurrences, not unique people. Everything is computed locally in your browser.
